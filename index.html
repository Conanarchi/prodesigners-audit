Why audit before you build

A clash fixed on site costs 2 to 5 times more than one fixed on paper.

Many private villas in Qatar are designed without a BIM workflow. Architectural, structural and MEP drawings are made separately and nobody checks them against each other, so the conflicts appear when the contractor is already pouring concrete.

Without an audit

  • Beams that cut through windows and door heads
  • Columns that land in the middle of a room
  • No space left for ducts and drainage above the ceiling
  • Rework, delays and disputes with the contractor

With ProDesigners

  • An independent review of every drawing you received
  • Clash detection that flags each conflict before construction
  • A clear report your designer and contractor can act on
  • Fast turnaround at a fee sized for private clients

What we check

Every discipline, checked against every other.

S-BEAM × A-OPENING

Beams through doors and windows

Beam depths that cut into door heads, windows and curtain walls.

S-COLUMN × A-WALL

Columns out of line

Columns that sit outside wall lines or land inside rooms and corridors.

MEP × STRUCTURE

Room for services

Ducts, drainage and pipes with no space between beams and the false ceiling.

STAIR × SLAB

Stairs and headroom

Headroom under 2.10 m, landings that hit beams, openings that don't match.

LEVELS & GRIDS

Levels that don't agree

Finished floor levels and grids that differ between architecture and structure.

C-SITE × BUILDING

Site and civil works

Boundary walls, ramps, pools, setbacks and drainage levels around the villa.

How it works

From your drawings to an action plan in one week.

  1. Today

    You share your drawings

    Upload the PDF or DWG files you received from your designer, from your phone or computer.

  2. Day 1–3

    We build the BIM model

    We model the critical elements of architecture and structure, plus site works.

  3. Day 3–5

    We run clash detection

    Automated tests find hard clashes, clearance problems and level mismatches.

  4. Day 5–7

    You get the audit report

    Each clash with 3D views, its priority and what to change. We walk you through it.

What you receive

A report your designer and contractor can act on.

  • Audit report (PDF) with a numbered list of clashes
  • 3D views and plan locations for each issue
  • Priority for each: critical, major or minor
  • A recommended fix for each issue
  • A review meeting to go through the findings

After the report you can go back to your original designer, or ask us to handle the redesign, approvals and interior design.

      Questions

      Before you send your drawings

      Which files should I send?

      Whatever your designer gave you: architectural and structural drawings as PDF or DWG, and MEP if you have it. Revit (RVT) and IFC models are welcome too. You can zip a whole folder and upload it in one go.

      Can I upload from my phone?

      Yes. Tap "Choose files" and pick them from your phone, iCloud, Google Drive or files you received on WhatsApp. For very large folders, paste a WeTransfer or Google Drive link instead.

      How much does it cost?

      A fixed fee per project, based on its size and the drawings you share. You receive the exact price by email or WhatsApp within 1–2 working days, with no commitment.

      How long does the audit take?

      About one week per villa from confirmation and receipt of complete drawings.

      Do you redesign or check structural calculations?

      The audit checks coordination between drawings. It does not include structural calculations or a full redesign. If you want us to fix the design, handle approvals or design the interiors, we quote that separately.

      Are my drawings safe?

      Your files are stored on ProDesigners' own servers and used only to prepare your audit and quotation. We don't share them with anyone.
